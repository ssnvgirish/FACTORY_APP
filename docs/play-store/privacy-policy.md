Prabitha QC ("the app") is an internal tool used by employees of [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us") to record production, quality, maintenance and salary-related reports for window frame and sheet manufacturing. The app is not offered to the public.

## 1. Information we collect

When you use the app we collect:

- **Account information:** your name, phone number, assigned machines, assigned roles and an account status flag. Administrators create these accounts.
- **Login credentials:** your password is stored by Firebase Authentication (Google) in a protected form. We do not see or store your password in readable form.
- **Work records:** the reports you submit, such as cleaning checks, tools counts, machine health and maintenance entries, production details, packing and quality results, customer rejections, and the salary calculations derived from them. Each record is tied to the machine, the shift, the date and the user who submitted it.
- **Technical data:** a server-side timestamp when a record is saved, and the Firebase App Check token that confirms the app is genuine.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, advertising identifiers, or browsing activity.

## 2. How we use information

We use this information to:

- let authorised staff sign in and submit reports;
- show production, efficiency and stock dashboards and salary calculations to the relevant staff;
- keep an audit trail of who submitted or changed a record;
- protect the service from unauthorised access.

## 3. Who can see information

Access is restricted by role. Operators see the reports relevant to their machines. Supervisors and administrators see the wider factory data and manage user accounts. Access to a user's account is controlled by the administrator.

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) in the Google Cloud region asia-south1. Google processes this data as our service provider. We do not sell personal information and do not share it with advertisers.

## 4. Retention

Work records are kept for as long as the company needs them for production, quality and payroll records. Account information is kept while the account exists. When an employee leaves, the administrator deactivates the account. To request deletion of an account or records, contact us (section 7). We may need to keep some records for legal or accounting reasons.

## 5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Firebase. Access rules in the database allow only signed-in users to read data, and only administrators to change user accounts and master settings. The app uses Firebase App Check to block requests from tampered or unofficial copies of the app.

## 6. Your choices

You can ask your administrator to correct your name, roles or assigned machines. Changing a phone number, which is also your login, requires the administrator to deactivate the old account and create a new one.
